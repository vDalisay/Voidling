# Voidling Demo MVP

Godot 4.6 C# vertical slice for the initial raising, breeding and racing loop.

- Editable Sprout Lands TileMap garden with LMB ground panning, zoom, Center reset and follow camera.
- Inline renaming by clicking the selected Voidling name; LMB hold/release pickup and placement.
- Menu-safe garden HUD; Stats/DNA/Visual Details separation; color-coded stats; Angel mutation/halo.
- Pannable scrollbar-free family tree.
- Shop, inventory, training, player-controlled breeding, world eggs/hatching, inbreeding and lineage persistence.
- One-player-vs-CPU side race with Run hurdles, Ocean Pack swimming animation, finite elevated Fly glide/fall-to-Swim behavior, Stamina/CHEER and speed streaks.
- Auto Finish ends once player placement is known; aligned podium/puddle results.
- Persistent volume and Auto Finish settings.

Use `playgame.bat` to run and `build.bat` to build. CI validates restore, C# build and Godot headless scene/project parsing.
